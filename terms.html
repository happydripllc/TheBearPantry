Orders & Reservations

Submitting the checkout form on this site reserves your requested items — it does not complete a sale or collect payment. Every order is confirmed personally, and payment is arranged directly with you at pickup or delivery.

Availability

All products are made in small, home-based batches, and availability changes often. We'll let you know as soon as possible if something you've requested isn't available.

Cottage Food Disclosure

The Bear Pantry is a home-based cottage food operation made in accordance with Texas Cottage Food Law. Products are made in a home kitchen that is not inspected by a government food safety agency.

Pickup & Delivery

Local pickup and, where available, local delivery are arranged after your order is confirmed. See Shipping & Pickup for details.
