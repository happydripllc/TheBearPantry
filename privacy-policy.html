Privacy Policy

This is a draft policy provided as a starting structure for The Bear Pantry. It should be reviewed by a qualified attorney before the site goes live to make sure it reflects your actual data practices and complies with applicable law.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, join our email list, or contact us, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, and any notes or messages you send us. We use this only to fulfill orders, respond to questions, and — if you opt in — send occasional updates about new products.

How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm and fulfill your order, including arranging pickup or delivery.
  • To respond to questions or requests you send us.
  • To send you email updates, but only if you've asked to join our list.

We do not sell your personal information.

Payment Information

The Bear Pantry does not currently collect or store payment information through this website. Payment is arranged directly with you (for example, cash, Venmo, or Cash App) at the time of pickup or delivery.

Cookies & Local Storage

This site uses your browser's local storage to remember what's in your shopping cart. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us until you submit an order form.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your information removed from our records, contact us.
